Now, what does Tobago's Requiem actually change?
As of 25 of November, 2026 it has:
3 new countries: Tobago, Jalisco, and Petoria
Expansion paths for Paraguay, Lithuania, Jalisco, and Tobago
A buffed Paraguay 
Events that allow you to choose between different government types for Jalisco AND the option to have Jalisco create a new national identity for it's people or reject independent national identity in favor of being considered Mexican

I hope to make more changes in the near future, specially polishing Jalisco and Petoria but also a way to create Rome if you play as the Papal States and a Muslim Caliphate
